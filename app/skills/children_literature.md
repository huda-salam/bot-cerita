# Children Literature Skill

Target: Indonesian children ages 7-10.

- Keep vocabulary concrete, natural and readable aloud.
- Give the child protagonist meaningful agency.
- Make conflict understandable without becoming simplistic.
- Prefer observable actions and dialogue over abstract emotional labels.
- Avoid gratuitous violence, humiliation or frightening detail.
- Escalate stakes through choices and consequences.
- End scenes with curiosity, emotional movement or a clear next objective.
- Preserve established character traits and facts.
