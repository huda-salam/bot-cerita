# Plot Design Skill

- Start with a specific protagonist desire and a concrete obstacle.
- Each scene must change the situation, knowledge, relationship or stakes.
- Escalate rather than repeat the same conflict.
- The climax should require a meaningful protagonist choice.
- Resolution should pay off the central premise and emotional arc.
- Avoid deus ex machina and unexplained reversals.
- Track setup, payoff and unresolved threads explicitly.
